S-I-Quotespro Privacy Policy

Updated November 18, 2026

S-I-Quotespro provides quoting and job management software for contractors. Contact support@s-i-quotespro.com or (713) 835-8245 about this policy or your data.

Information used by the service

We process account identifiers, name, email, company details, role and sign-in information. Your workspace may contain customer names, phone numbers, email addresses, job addresses, photos, scopes of work, labor rates, quotes, signatures, invoices, recorded payments, schedules and notes. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication.

When you use property lookup, the service processes the address you enter and, if you permit it, your device location to estimate travel and property details. Notification identifiers are used when notifications are configured and you enable them. Error reports may contain the page address, technical error, browser information and account identifier to diagnose problems.

Purpose and service providers

We use this information to authenticate accounts, keep companies' workspaces separate, synchronize records, calculate quotes, generate documents, send enabled alerts, assist support and diagnose errors. Google Firebase provides authentication, database and file storage. Vercel hosts server functions. Anthropic processes the job details and photos supplied to AI quoting features. Google Maps services process address and location information for property lookup. Existing web subscriptions are processed by Stripe. The Android app provides access to an existing account and does not offer subscription checkout.

Information is disclosed to these providers to perform the features you use, to the authorized members of your company workspace, and to recipients you choose when sharing a document. Service providers may process information outside your home country. We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising in this app.

Device access and security

Camera or photo selection is used when you attach job images. Location access is optional. Sharing and saving files use the Android system dialogs. The app uses encrypted network connections. Access to company records depends on sign-in, workspace membership and role. You can revoke permissions in Android Settings.

Retention and deletion

Records are retained to provide the service while the account or company workspace is active. You can request deletion of your account and associated personal data using the Delete account page. We verify ownership before deleting an account. We may retain information needed for accounting, legal obligations, security or dispute resolution. Company business records involving other people may remain with the company where it has a legitimate retention need. We explain any applicable retention when processing your request. Cached app data can also be removed in Android Settings, although clearing device data does not delete cloud records.

Your choices

Contact support to request access, correction, export or deletion of your personal information. You can sign out, revoke device permissions, choose whether to use AI tools and select the recipients of exported documents. The app is intended for adult contractors and their authorized team members.
