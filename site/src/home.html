How it works

From lead to quote in three steps

STEP 1 · 5 MINUTES, ONCE

Connect your lead inbox

Sign up, then copy your personal lead script into Google. From then on, every Thumbtack, Angi, HomeAdvisor, Yelp or Bark email that lands in your Gmail is picked up within about 5 minutes.

STEP 2 · AUTOMATIC

AI drafts the quote

It reads the request and the photos, writes the scope, sizes the crew, lists the materials and prices the job with your labor rate, overhead and margin. It drafts a reply to the customer too.

STEP 3 · YOUR CALL

You check it and send

Adjust anything, then send a branded PDF. Prefer hands-off? Turn on auto-reply and customers get your answer by email as soon as the lead arrives.

Help center

Need a hand? We pick up.

Connect Thumbtack in 5 steps

  1. In S-I-Quotespro, open Settings → Lead inbox and tap Copy my lead script.
  2. Go to script.google.com and choose New project.
  3. Delete what's in the editor, paste your script, and click Save.
  4. Choose setup next to Debug and click Run.
  5. Allow access: pick your account → Advanced → Go to project → Allow. The log shows ✅ CONNECTED within a few seconds.

Do I have to download an app?

No. Open s-i-quotespro.com on your phone and use "Add to Home Screen". It works like an app, with phone alerts, and updates on its own.

Does it send quotes to customers without me?

Only if you turn on auto-reply and the lead includes the customer's email. Otherwise every quote waits for you to check it and send it.

Where do the prices come from?

From your own labor rate, overhead and profit margin, which you set in Settings. The AI estimates the work and materials; the price is calculated with your numbers, and it shows a market estimate next to it for comparison.

Why does Google say the script isn't verified?

The lead script runs inside your own Google account, so Google shows a warning for any script you add yourself. Choose Advanced, then Go to project. It only reads lead emails and sends them to your S-I-Quotespro workspace. You can stop it any time by running stopConnector.

Can my crew use it?

Yes. Give them your team code. They sign up as "New team member", you approve them, and you can require your approval before their quotes go out.

How do I cancel or change my plan?

Open Settings → Plan & billing → Manage billing. You can change your card, switch plans, see invoices or cancel. Canceling during the trial means you're never charged.

What is my Support ID?

A 6-character code under the Help button in the app. Read it to us when you call and we'll find your account right away.
